## Localization

The interface, the eight place names, the title mark and the web app manifest
were translated into Simplified Chinese; the original English comments in the
source were left as they are. In Chinese the title is rendered 水中 所藏 so the
last two characters sink into the water, as "keeps" does in the original mark.
Chinese text uses the system's serif faces (Songti SC, Noto Serif CJK SC and
similar), so the mirror adds no font download for it.

## Changes from the original page

The original is a static site with no analytics, accounts or online services.
This mirror keeps its own copy of every asset and makes no cross-origin
requests. The original share card and icons are retained; the mirror's Open
Graph image points to the archive's own screenshot. The development-only
screenshot and census hooks in the original source are left in place but are
inert for visitors.

A slow first-person boat ride through eight places, from dusk to dawn; no
score and no losing. Supports keyboard/mouse, touch and gamepad.
